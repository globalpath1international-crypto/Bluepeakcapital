How to Deposit

1
Choose your asset and network. Pick the cryptocurrency you want to deposit, then select the correct network.
2
Tap "Generate Deposit Address". A unique deposit address will appear. Copy it or scan the QR code.
3
Send crypto from your external wallet. Use the same asset and network you selected. Sending a different asset or network may cause permanent loss.
4
Wait for confirmation. Your deposit will be credited to your wallet after the required network confirmations.
💡 Important: Always double-check the asset and network before sending. Deposits sent on the wrong network cannot be recovered.
